## Identified inconsistency

The original protocol correctly freezes C0 as a trace-only comparator.

For every scenario C0 must:

- terminate `INCONCLUSIVE`;
- emit no specific diagnosis;
- execute zero diagnostic replay experiments;
- consume zero replay-cost units;
- record zero replay workflow steps.

The original protocol also states generically that after an arm finishes,
K3C2 must replay the arm's final observation history through
`select_next_open_set_decision` and require agreement with the arm result.

Those two requirements are incompatible for C0.

C0 intentionally does not enter the active diagnostic lifecycle.

Its final observation history is empty.

The same zero-observation diagnosis state is the initial selectable state from
which the active arms begin ordinary diagnosis.

Therefore C0's intentional trace-only `INCONCLUSIVE` result must not be tested
as though it were the terminal output of the active open-set lifecycle.

This is a protocol-scope defect, not an observed treatment result.

---

## Corrected terminal lifecycle scope

The frozen terminal lifecycle consistency check applies to:

- C1 `seeded_random_active`;
- C2 `max_information_gain_active`;
- C3 `heal_information_gain_per_cost`.

It does not apply to C0 `trace_only`.

For C1-C3, after an arm finishes, K3C2 must call the frozen
`select_next_open_set_decision` on that arm's final observation history.

The independently recomputed decision must agree with the arm result on:

- terminal status;
- diagnosed hypothesis, when any;
- open-set terminal reason.
