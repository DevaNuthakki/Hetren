registered deterministic controlled mechanism experiment.

- primary H1 criterion: PASS;

**KEEP the cost-aware active-diagnosis mechanism as a supported foundation
component.**

## Resource interpretation

The registered costs in P09.3 are synthetic experiment-cost units.

C3 and C2 each consumed 192 total synthetic actual cost units overall.

C3 used 192 experiment executions and 768 workflow steps.

C2 used 112 experiment executions and 448 workflow steps.

Therefore P09.3 does not establish lower real compute use, fewer tool calls,
lower latency, lower monetary cost, or greater production efficiency for C3.

The supported result concerns diagnosis quality under the registered
synthetic cost construction.

## Why the next experiment must be harder

Repeating the same synthetic construction would add little scientific value.

The next validation layer should test whether the supported diagnosis
mechanism survives substantially richer controlled agent behavior.

The next experiment should increase ecological difficulty while retaining
enough evaluator control to know the latent failure and score outcomes
without leaking that truth into treatment.

---

## Handoff to P09.4

The next planned phase is:

**P09.4 — Richer Controlled Multi-Step Agent Validation Design**

P09.4 starts as a design and preregistration task, not as an execution task.

Before any protected P09.4 result is produced, the design should address at
least:

1. longer multi-step trajectories rather than the compact P09.3 mechanism;
2. state that changes across tool or workflow actions;
3. multiple plausible competing failure explanations;
4. interventions whose observations emerge from workflow execution rather
   than direct hidden-label lookup;
5. richer fault families such as tool selection, parameter transformation,
   orchestration, state/context, environment interaction, and validation
   weakness where technically appropriate;
6. explicit treatment/evaluator information-flow separation;
7. observable-information parity between comparison arms;
8. fair and preregistered budgets;
9. development cases separate from protected cases;
10. hidden evaluator truth isolated from treatment;
11. negative controls and ablations defined before protected results;
12. deterministic reproducibility where appropriate;
13. explicit invalid-run and no-rerun-to-win rules;
14. claim boundaries written before outcome inspection;
15. preservation of P09.3 as immutable prior evidence.

P09.4 must not assume that the P09.3 effect size will reproduce.

A weaker, null, mixed, or unfavorable P09.4 result remains scientifically
valid and must be retained.

## Final closure

P09.3 is complete.

Its contribution is bounded but meaningful:

a preregistered, held-out, one-shot controlled experiment supports the
cost-aware active-diagnosis mechanism under the exact frozen synthetic
construction.

The correct engineering and scientific response is not to inflate this
result.

The correct response is to preserve it and test the mechanism under a harder
validation layer.
