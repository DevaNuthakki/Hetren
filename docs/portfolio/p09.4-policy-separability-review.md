## Review interpretation

A2C6I2 establishes a narrow but important mechanism fact:

**C2 and C3 are policy-separable in the frozen deterministic development
problem under the calibrated replay-cost catalogue.**

The cost catalogue changes experiment selection on valid reachable diagnosis
states.

The equal-cost control returning C3 to the C2 choice supports attribution of
the observed selection differences to the calibrated cost asymmetry within this
development mechanism.

---
