## 4. Product And Research Boundaries

Hetren is one product built around a shared Core and shared evidence,
execution, permission, verification, memory, artifact, and release semantics.

Hetren maintains two distinct but connected intelligence research programs.

### 4.1 Native Hetren Intelligence

Native Intelligence investigates whether a Hetren-developed trainable system
can learn useful system understanding, experiment selection, outcome
prediction, method construction, engineering decisions, stopping behavior,
and transfer from audited engineering trajectories.

Research documentation belongs under:

`docs/research/native_intelligence/`

Research implementation belongs under:

`research/native_intelligence/`

### 4.2 Adaptive Intelligence

Adaptive Intelligence investigates controlled, reversible, and verifiable
post-deployment adaptation, including Hetren Governed Plasticity, locality,
retention, rollback, sequential learning, consolidation, transfer, and
Experience Capsules.

Research documentation belongs under:

`docs/research/adaptive_intelligence/`

Research implementation belongs under:

`research/adaptive_intelligence/`

### 4.3 Shared Integration Boundary

Native Intelligence and Adaptive Intelligence may use the same canonical
Hetren contracts and infrastructure, including:

- GoalContract;
- SystemSnapshot;
- CapabilityProfile;
- MethodProposal;
- ExecutionRecord;
- EvaluationRecord;
- CandidateChange;
- VerificationReport;
- ExperienceRecord and ReleaseRecord;
- LearningTrajectory;
- ModelRelease;
- Artifact Graph;
- Action and Permission Gateway;
- private execution;
- independent verification.

Separate research programs must not create duplicate Hetren Cores, permission
systems, evidence semantics, or verification authorities merely for
convenience.

Experimental research code becomes product capability only through explicit
qualification and release decisions.

## 5. Initial MVP Scope

The initial cross-domain qualification program requires:

1. a tool-using agent environment;
2. a structured-data ML environment;
3. a vision environment.

Both Behavioral and authorized Internal Engines are first-class MVP
capabilities.

A Hetren-developed native neural candidate must be implemented, trained,
ablated, and evaluated.

Adaptive Intelligence begins in parallel through research governance,
baseline reproduction, protected qualification design, rollback and locality
testing, and bounded HGP experiments.

Failure of the adaptive scientific hypothesis does not by itself invalidate
the engineering product.

A positive adaptive result does not waive product qualification, independent
verification, permissions, recovery, compatibility, or rollback.

## 7. Evidence And Change-Control Rule

Specifications establish direction and requirements.

Repository implementation, executed tests, qualification records,
experimental manifests, protected evaluation, verification evidence, and
release records establish implementation or scientific status.

A product result does not establish an adaptive-intelligence claim.

An adaptive-intelligence result does not establish general product
qualification.

Scientific hypotheses are not facts because they appear in a research plan.

Material changes to product scope, architecture, hypotheses, primary metrics,
protected data, baselines, qualification thresholds, or release requirements
must be recorded through the applicable decision-governance process.

Protected success criteria must not be silently changed after protected
results are observed.

Historical evidence and superseded decisions must remain traceable.
